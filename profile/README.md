## About Normify

Regulatory requirements are constantly evolving. Keeping track of applicable laws, standards and guidelines is complex, time-consuming and essential for every organization.

**Normify makes regulatory intelligence accessible and actionable.**

We build technology that helps organizations:

- **Discover** relevant laws, regulations, guidelines and standards.
- **Monitor** regulatory changes and updates over time.
- **Understand** requirements and their implications.
- **Analyze** processes against applicable compliance requirements.
- **Extract** structured information from regulatory and technical documents.
- **Integrate** regulatory intelligence into existing systems through APIs.

Our goal is to reduce the complexity of regulatory compliance through intelligent data processing, automation and accessible interfaces.

## What we're building

Our platform consists of specialized services and APIs that make regulatory information easier to access, process and apply.

### Regulatory Intelligence

Discover and search relevant regulatory information.

- [legal-discover-api](https://github.com/Normify-me/legal-discover-api) – Identify relevant laws, regulations, guidelines and standards based on natural language input.
- [legal-search-api](https://github.com/Normify-me/legal-search-api) – Search laws, standards and norms in the Normify database.
- [legal-update-api](https://github.com/Normify-me/legal-update-api) – Detect changes and updates to laws and standards.

### Compliance & Process Analysis

Make regulatory requirements actionable.

- [process-compliance-check-api](https://github.com/Normify-me/process-compliance-check-api) – Analyze process diagrams and descriptions against applicable management system standards and regulatory requirements.

### Document Intelligence

Turn complex technical documents into structured information.

- [hazardous-substances-api](https://github.com/Normify-me/hazardous-substances-api) – Extract hazardous substance information, classifications and relevant properties from safety data sheets (PDF).

## Our Approach

We believe that regulatory compliance should not be a manual research exercise.

Our approach combines:

1. **Structured regulatory data** – Making laws, standards and regulations accessible in a machine-readable way.
2. **Intelligent discovery** – Finding applicable requirements from contextual and natural language input.
3. **Automated analysis** – Connecting regulatory requirements with products, documents and business processes.
4. **Continuous monitoring** – Keeping track of changes in the regulatory landscape.
5. **API-first architecture** – Enabling seamless integration into existing software and business workflows.
